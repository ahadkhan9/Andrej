21. My Learning Journal

Why should I care?

Reproducing models involves significant trial-and-error. Logging your system performance measurements, learning rate configurations, overfitting outcomes, and troubleshooting notes provides a valuable personal reference for future development work.

Backend Engineer Lens

Think of this journal like a system performance runbook or post-mortem log. When an outage occurs, you write a post-mortem to document what broke, why it happened, how it was resolved, and preventative measures. Similarly, you log model training runs, throughput speeds, and debugging findings here to track optimization progress.

My Lessons Learned & Mistakes

  • Mistake: Initializing the model directly on the GPU/MPS device.
    Lesson: Always initialize model weights on the CPU first before transferring to guarantee identical weight initializations across different hardware backends.
  • Mistake: Forgetting to divide micro-batch loss by grad_accum_steps during accumulation.
    Lesson: This causes gradients to sum up unchecked, effectively scaling the learning rate by $N$ and destabilizing training.
